My Dear Apurva,

22 November.

A date that was special long before I became a part of your life. A date that brought you into this world, into your family, into the lives of all the people who have loved you, cared for you, watched you grow, and helped you become the person you are today. And now, somehow, this date has become special to me too. ❤️

This year, your birthday feels a little different.

Perhaps because when I wish you today, I am not simply wishing a girl I am going to marry. I am wishing the woman who, in just a few days, will become my wife, my companion, my friend, my family, and one of the most important people in my life. 💍❤️

Your excitement, your smile, that feeling of accomplishment when something you have hoped for finally becomes real — these are the moments I want to share with you. 🥹💖

I want to be there when you achieve something big, celebrating with you as if it were my own achievement. I also want to be there on the days when things don't go as planned, when you feel tired, when you doubt yourself or when you need someone to remind you of how capable you are. ❤️🫂

Because supporting you doesn't mean standing beside you only when everything is going well. It also means reminding you of your strength when you temporarily forget it yourself.

And yes, madam ji, now that the Silver Play Button has arrived, I suppose the Gold Play Button will have to be the next target! 🤭😂🥇 I'll be right here cheering you on, celebrating every milestone and occasionally reminding you to take a break when you get too busy chasing your dreams. 😜❤️

Your dreams are important to me, but you are even more important than any milestone you achieve.

I will always be proud of what you accomplish, but my love for you will never depend on how successful you become or how many awards you receive.

Whether you are celebrating your next big achievement or simply having an ordinary day, you will always have someone who believes in you, appreciates you and wants to see you happy. 🫂❤️🧿

So here's to your Silver Play Button, your hard work, your dreams and every beautiful achievement that is yet to come.

Congratulations, my love! I'm so proud of you. 🥈🏆❤️

And one day, when we look back at this chapter of your life, I hope we smile at how many beautiful things you accomplished and how many more we experienced together. 🥹❤️

Chapter IX

Two families, one home

Apurva, as your birthday approaches, I keep thinking about the strange and beautiful way life works.

On 22 November, years ago, your family celebrated the arrival of a daughter.

And on 24 November, this year, my family will celebrate the arrival of that same daughter into our home. 🥹❤️

And I don't want to think of it as you leaving one family and joining another.

I want to think of it as two families becoming one bigger family. 🧿❤️

Because I know that you have a family that has loved you long before I ever entered your life.

They watched you grow.They celebrated your achievements.They worried about you.They stood beside you.They made you who you are.

And I will always respect that.

I don't want to replace the home you came from.

I want to become another home for you. 🏡❤️
